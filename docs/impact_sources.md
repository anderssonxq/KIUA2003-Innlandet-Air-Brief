## Proposed contribution

The pilot addresses information preparation in one Innlandet municipality:
a person preparing a dated Hamar update obtains a Norwegian draft with the
municipality, forecast reference time, local-day coverage, selected facts and
source links preserved. Information staff and activity organisers are proposed
users, not organisations that have requested or adopted the system.

The value hypothesis is less manual transcription and easier evidence checking.
No time saving, user demand, health benefit or change in behaviour has been
measured. The source documents establish a local context, not demand for this
particular product. Existing official information remains an important baseline.

## Boundaries

- County-wide background conditions and local winter episodes are different
  geographic and temporal measures; they are not contradictory observations.
- The pilot does not establish a general county-wide pollution crisis or address
  every major problem in Innlandet.
- Station measurements and historical particulate observations do not validate
  the municipal AQI forecast used by this workflow.
- The bulletin does not decide whether an individual's activity is safe, assess
  regulatory compliance, direct dust-control operations or replace official alerts.
- A useful local task may justify the project; adding an LLM still needs a
  comparison with simpler approaches.
